# Design System: FieldNote

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Exhibit Deck"**

Every panel in FieldNote is a numbered Exhibit in a consulting deck: a 2px navy rule across the top, a run-in "Exhibit n" set in condensed bold, an action-title sentence that states the conclusion, the figure or chart, and a Source line beneath a hairline. Trust is the grammar of the page. The reader scans titles, checks sources, and drills into evidence without leaving the page.

The material is paper and ink. A white ground (dark mode: deep blue-black paper), navy ink for text, rules and primary actions, steel blue for secondary series and links, and one rust accent kept for the highlighted series, the top rank and the active marker. Structure comes from rules, not boxes: hairlines separate, the 2px exhibit rule opens, and in-flow surfaces carry no shadow. Corners are nearly square (2px). One variable family, Archivo, does all the work. Its width axis tightens numerals and headings, and tabular figures keep columns aligned. Superscript footnote markers tie numbers to their Source lines.

Density is operational: built for a laptop morning scan, with generous exhibit spacing (52px) and tight controls (28 to 36px). Motion is quick and informative, never decorative. Charts draw in once from their baseline, the Board re-ranks with FLIP, and indicators slide between states. Routine transitions stay in the 120 to 320ms band.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Numbered Exhibits with a run-in number, an action title and a Source line
- Rules instead of cards: a 2px navy exhibit rule, 1px hairlines, no shadows on in-flow surfaces
- One rust accent, reserved for the highlight
- Five reserved status markings: verified, thin evidence, disputed, stale, fixture
- Archivo Variable with the width axis and tabular lining figures
- Fast, ease-out motion that draws in once, with a user Motion setting over prefers-reduced-motion

## Colors

A cool paper-and-ink palette: navy and steel for structure and data, one warm rust accent for emphasis, and a separate set of status hues that are never used for decoration.

### Primary
- **Exhibit Navy** (`navy`, `ink-strong`, `rule-strong`, `action`): the ink of record. Used for headings, figure values, the 2px exhibit rule, table header rules, primary buttons, slider tracks and checkbox accents. In dark mode it flips to a pale blue-grey (`navy-dark`, `action-dark`) so primary actions become light-on-dark.

### Secondary
- **Survey Steel** (`steel`, with `steel-2` and `steel-3` as lighter tints): secondary series, links, footnote markers, evidence numbers, disclosure toggles and the focus colour (`focus`). The input focus halo is steel at 18%.

### Tertiary
- **Exhibit Rust** (`rust`, `rust-soft`): the one highlight. It marks the highlighted chart series, the rank-1 numeral on findings and the Board, the 1px active marker on the current nav item, the active tab underline, the text caret, and the negative end of the diverging scale. It is never used as a fill for surfaces or buttons.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (`paper`, `paper-raised`): page ground and raised controls (buttons, inputs, selected chips, indicator thumbs).
- **Paper Two / Three** (`paper-2`, `paper-3`): sidebar, segmented-control well, weights panel, "how to execute" boxes (`paper-2`); bar tracks, skeletons, inline code (`paper-3`).
- **Ink ramp** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): body text, secondary labels and memo terms, and tertiary metadata including Source lines and chart axis text.
- **Rules** (`rule`, `rule-soft`): 1px hairlines between figures, findings, table rows and evidence items. `rule-soft` is for rows inside a list.
- **Context grey** (`context`): unhighlighted series, entities outside the eight-slot palette, scrollbar thumbs, input hover borders.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`): the barely-there hover wash on table rows, Board rows and quiet buttons.

### Data palettes
- **Entity categorical** (`c1` to `c8`): steel, ochre, sage, violet, cyan, rose, olive, periwinkle. Slots are assigned by the workspace's config order and never by rank, so an entity keeps its colour on every chart and filter. Entities past the eighth slot fall back to `context`.
- **Criterion ramp** (`crit-1` to `crit-4`): a navy-to-pale-steel sequence for market size, competitor gap, evidence strength and effort, used in the Board's stacked contribution bars and the weight keys. In dark mode the ramp runs light-to-dark (`crit-1-dark` is lightest).
- **Diverging** (`div-neg`, `div-mid`, `div-pos`): rust to neutral to steel, for sentiment and deltas.
- **Diff** (`ins`, `del`): green and rust washes for inserted and deleted text in change diffs.

### Status markings (reserved)
- **Verified** (`verified`, `verified-soft`): critic-checked claims, healthy run dot, upward Board movement, success notes.
- **Thin evidence** (`thin`, `thin-soft`): demoted findings, partial or running runs, warning figures and notes.
- **Disputed** (`disputed`, `disputed-soft`): disputed claims, counter-evidence, failed runs, error notes, destructive buttons.
- **Stale** (`stale`): aged findings. Shown as a grey mark with a **dashed** border and no fill.
- **Fixture** (`fixture`, `fixture-bg`, `fixture-hatch`): demo data. Amber ink on a warm ground. The page-level fixture band adds a 45° hatch (6px stripes).

### Named Rules
**The One Rust Rule.** Rust marks the single thing the reader should see first: the highlighted series, rank 1, the active marker. If two things on a screen are rust, one of them is wrong.

**The Reserved Status Rule.** Verified, thin, disputed, stale and fixture hues mean only those states. Never use them for decoration, category or branding.

**The Stable Entity Rule.** Entity colour comes from config order through the `c1` to `c8` slots and stays the same on every chart, legend and filter on every page.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Variable (with Archivo, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif), loaded with the width axis.
**Body Font:** Archivo Variable, the same family.
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace (Cascadia Mono, SF Mono, Consolas) for code, logs and config editors only.

**Character:** One grotesque family that changes register through the width axis instead of pairing fonts. Numerals and headings run condensed (wdth 78 to 94), body runs at normal width (wdth 100), so a page reads like a typeset deck and not like a dashboard.

The rem scale is 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 20, 24, 30 and 40px (`0.6875rem` to `2.5rem`). Headings default to weight 650 at line-height 1.2 with balanced wrapping. Paragraphs use pretty wrapping.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (640, 1.875rem, 1.16, wdth 94, -0.02em): the page's action-title sentence, capped at 44ch. Drops to 1.5rem under 720px.
- **Figure** (600, 1.875rem, 1.05, wdth 88, tabular): numbers in the figure band. Units are set at 0.55em in `ink-2`.
- **Rank** (280, 2.5rem, 0.9, wdth 78, tabular): the hanging rank numeral on memo entries. The Board uses a 1.5rem, weight 300, wdth 80 variant.
- **Headline** (620, 1.1875rem, 1.3, -0.01em): finding titles, max 60ch.
- **Title** (560, 1.0625rem, 1.35): exhibit action titles, balanced.
- **Exhibit number** (750, 0.8125rem, wdth 86): the run-in "Exhibit n" before the title.
- **Body** (400, 0.9375rem, 1.55): running text. Memo paragraphs stop at 74ch and page decks at 78ch.
- **Label** (620, 0.8125rem): field labels, memo terms (650), weight heads, segmented options (600).
- **Source** (400, 0.78rem, 1.55, `ink-3`): Source and Note lines, with "Source:" bolded in `ink-2`.
- **Caption** (400, 0.75rem, 1.4): figure subs, hints, marks (620), table headers (650), chart legends.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every number that can sit next to another number (figures, scores, tables, ranks, marks, chart text) uses `tabular-nums lining-nums`.

**The Width Axis Rule.** Emphasis on numbers and headings comes from narrowing the width axis (78 to 94), not from switching families or adding colour.

**The Footnote Rule.** A figure that has a source carries a superscript steel marker (0.68em, weight 700) that matches its numbered Source line.

## Layout

A two-column shell: a 256px sticky contents rail on `paper-2` with a 1px right rule, and a main column holding an optional fixture band, a sticky 48px run header (paper at 94% over the content, hairline beneath), and the page. Pages are capped at 1200px of content with 48px side gutters and 44px top padding.

The vertical rhythm is Exhibit-driven. Exhibits sit 52px apart (36px for the first after the page head), with 12px from the rule to the head, 20px from the head to the body, and 18px to the Source line. The figure band is an auto-fit grid (min 168px) of columns divided by hairlines with 22px inner padding. Memo findings use a 52px rank column, a fluid body and a 232px score panel with 32px gaps. Memo entries are a 148px term column beside the definition.

Breakpoints: at 1180px the score panel moves under the finding body and the weights grid drops to two columns. At 960px the rail becomes a 280px off-canvas drawer (320ms ease-out slide, with a scrim), a mobile bar appears, gutters shrink to 16px, and Board rows restack to rank, title and total over a full-width bar. At 720px the display size drops, the figure band goes to two columns, memo terms stack over definitions, and diffs stack.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat. Depth comes from rules and paper tone, not shadow. In-flow surfaces (exhibits, figures, findings, tables, Board rows, panels) never cast a shadow. Only layers that float above the page lift.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Pop** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.22), 0 2px 6px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.08)`; dark `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0,0,0,0.6), 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)`): chart tooltips, the workspace menu, Ask popovers, toasts, and the mobile drawer.
- **Indicator lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.06 to 0.08)`): only on the sliding nav highlight and the segmented thumb, so the moving indicator reads as a physical tab.
- **Focus halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px` steel at 18%, or 30% on slider thumbs): input and range focus. All other focus uses a 2px `focus` outline with a 2px offset.

### Named Rules
**The Rules-Not-Cards Rule.** Group content with a rule above it, never by boxing it in a shadowed card. If a panel needs separation, give it a hairline or a `paper-2` ground.

## Shapes

Nearly square. The base radius is 2px (`rounded.sm`) for buttons, inputs, chips, marks, notes, the nav highlight and panels. Nested indicators (segmented options and thumb, slider thumbs, colour swatches) use 1px so they sit inside their 2px frame. Only the workspace menu uses 4px. Circles are limited to status dots and spinners. Bars (criterion bars, contribution bars, chart bars) are square-ended.

Lines carry the form: the 2px exhibit rule in `rule-strong` opens each exhibit, 1px hairlines divide, 2px range tracks and a 2px rust tab underline mark position, and stale marks use a dashed border.

## Components

### Exhibit (signature)
The unit of every page. A `figure` with a 2px `rule-strong` top border. The caption runs in "Exhibit n" (exhibit-number type) directly into the action-title sentence, with optional tools (segmented control, chips) aligned right. Its footer is the Source line: a 1px hairline, then "Source:" and an optional "Note:" in source type, max 120ch. Exhibits are numbered in page order.

### Figure band
Exhibit 1 on Overview. Up to five figures in hairline-divided columns: label (13px, 550, `ink-2`), value (figure type, count-up over 550ms on change), optional superscript footnote marker, and a caption-sized sub-line. Warning and bad values take `thin` and `disputed` ink.

### Memo finding entry
A ruled list item. The hanging rank numeral is light and condensed (rust at weight 400 for rank 1). The headline follows, then a marks row, then a definition list with "What happened", "Why it matters" (with a small "Analysis" claim-type tag) and "Recommended action". Counter-evidence appears in `disputed`. A steel disclosure ("Evidence (n) and how to execute") reveals a `paper-2` how-to box and a numbered evidence list with hairline-bordered quotes. The right score panel shows the weighted total (1.5rem, wdth 88) and four criterion bars (4px, `paper-3` track) that draw in when first seen.

### Marks
21px inline labels with a 2px radius, 12px weight 620 tabular text, and a 12px line icon at stroke 2.2. They come in five reserved kinds: verified (shield-check), thin (triangle-alert), disputed (circle-slash), stale (clock, dashed border) and fixture. Each takes its status ink, a border of that ink at 35 to 40%, and its soft fill. The neutral mark takes `ink-2` on a `rule` border. The plain mark is borderless `ink-3` metadata ("3 sources").

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 2px radius, 34px high (28px small), weight 620.
- **Primary:** `action` fill with `action-ink` text. Hover goes to `action-hover`.
- **Secondary:** `paper-raised` with a `rule` border. Hover darkens the border to `ink-3`.
- **Quiet:** transparent steel text. Hover shows a `scrim` wash with `ink-strong` text.
- **Danger:** `disputed` text, with a disputed border on hover.
- **Active:** all buttons press down 1px (100ms). Disabled is 45% opacity.

### Chips
Filter toggles, 30px high with a 2px radius. Off: transparent with `ink-3` text and the entity swatch at 35%. On: `paper-raised` with an `ink-3` border, `ink-strong` text and a 2px inset `ink-strong` underline. Colour transitions take 120ms.

### Segmented control
A `paper-2` well with a 1px rule and 2px padding holding 28px options. The selected option sits on a `paper-raised` thumb (1px radius, indicator lift) that slides between options through a shared layout id (spring stiffness 520, damping 42). It is used for exhibit views, Theme and the Motion setting.

### Inputs / Fields
36px, `paper-raised`, 1px `rule` border, 2px radius, 14px text. Hover goes to a `context` border. Focus goes to a `steel` border with a 3px halo at 18% and no outline. Invalid gets a `disputed` border. The caret is rust everywhere. Range sliders use a 2px track filled navy to the value and a 1px-radius square thumb with a 2px navy border that scales to 1.2 while dragging.

### Navigation
The contents rail groups pages under 12px weight 650 group labels. Items are 14px weight 500 in `ink-2`, with 16px line icons. The active item goes to `ink-strong` at 620 and sits on a raised highlight with a 1px rust marker on its left edge. The highlight slides between items through a shared layout id (spring stiffness 560, damping 44). Page tabs (Workspaces) use the same device: a 2px rust underline that slides with layout id `tab-line` (spring 520/42).

### Board row (signature)
A grid row: rank, title with marks, a 14px stacked contribution bar in criterion colours (segments split by a 1px paper inset), total (three decimals, weight 700), sources, last seen, chevron. Rank 1 is rust. When weights change, rows re-flow by FLIP (layout position, spring stiffness 380, damping 36, mass 0.9). The rank numeral rolls 12px in the direction of travel (220ms). A small up or down move count appears in `verified` or `rust` (180ms). Contribution segments morph width over 420ms ease-out. A row expands into the same memo definition list.

### Fixture band
A full-width band above the run header whenever fixture data is shown: `fixture-bg` with a 45° hatch, `fixture` ink, bold lead-in and a hairline border in fixture at 30%. It is never dismissible.

### Motion grammar
- **Durations:** fast 120ms (hover, colour), base 200ms (disclosure chevrons), slow 320ms (drawer). Entrances run 180 to 280ms. Chart draw-in runs 550 to 800ms.
- **Curve:** ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for nearly everything. Ease-in-out `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` is available for symmetric moves.
- **Page entry:** content rises 10px and un-blurs 2px over 280ms.
- **Draw-in once:** charts, criterion bars and contribution bars grow from their baseline the first time they scroll into view (40px bottom margin). The stagger is capped at 0.3s. If an exhibit has not been seen after 1.2s it is shown at rest instantly, so no exhibit is ever empty.
- **Indicators:** nav highlight, segmented thumb and tab underline move by shared layout id on stiff springs.
- **Settings:** Motion is Full / Less / Auto in the rail. Auto follows `prefers-reduced-motion`. Less or a reduce preference sets Motion's `reducedMotion` to always, zeroes durations, removes the page-entry offset, and replaces rank roll with a fade. CSS animations clamp to 1ms under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every panel in a numbered Exhibit with a run-in "Exhibit n", an action-title sentence that states the conclusion, and a Source line.
- **Do** separate with a 2px `rule-strong` exhibit rule and 1px hairlines, and keep in-flow surfaces flat.
- **Do** keep corners at 2px, with 1px for nested indicators.
- **Do** set every comparable number in tabular lining figures, and narrow numerals and headings with the width axis.
- **Do** tie sourced figures to their Source line with a superscript steel footnote marker.
- **Do** assign entity colours from `c1` to `c8` by config order, and fall back to `context`.
- **Do** keep transitions between 120 and 320ms on the ease-out curve, draw charts in once, and honour the Motion setting and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** label fixture data with the hatched fixture band and fixture marks on every page it appears.

### Don't:
- **Don't** build KPI-card grids or coloured cards. Use the figure band and ruled lists instead.
- **Don't** put shadows on in-flow surfaces. Pop is for floating layers only.
- **Don't** use rust for more than one highlight per view, or as a surface or button fill.
- **Don't** use verified, thin, disputed, stale or fixture hues for anything but those states.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface for display. Archivo's width axis is the display voice.
- **Don't** replay chart draw-ins on re-render, or leave an exhibit empty while it waits for scroll.
