## Scope and mode

Whole FieldNote web app (replaces the Streamlit dashboard): Overview, Market & competitors, Metrics, Customer voice,
Opportunity Board, Ask FieldNote, Briefs, Actions, Run Inspector, Evals, Workspaces. Mode: Operate.
Audience: a strategy lead's morning scan on a laptop, occasional deep sessions. Guardrails from the user: no hype
or crypto look, no slow motion, light and dark both work.

## Direction contract

THESIS: every panel is a numbered Exhibit with an action-title sentence and a Source line, so trust is the grammar
of the page. Refuses the category default of a KPI-card grid over coloured cards.

OWN-WORLD: consulting exhibit deck. White paper ground, navy ink (#0E2A47) for text, rules and primary actions,
steel blue for secondary series, one rust accent (#B3452C) reserved for the highlighted series and the "so what".
Hairline rules, no card shadows, square corners (2px). One family (Archivo, width axis) with tabular figures;
superscript footnote markers tie numbers to Source lines. Status markings are reserved: verified, thin evidence,
disputed, stale, fixture.

STORY: the lead reads each page's action title, scans the exhibit, checks the source line, and drills into
evidence or acts (re-weight, ask, assign, run) without leaving the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Overview. Left contents rail (workspace switcher, pages). Page head: action-title sentence built
from data, run line under it. Exhibit 1 is a five-figure band with footnotes; below, ranked findings as memo
entries with hanging rank numerals, labelled paragraphs, and an evidence disclosure.

FORM: Exhibit & Source Line, position 1 of the ordered grounded list. The roll (seed key e2bf5baa,
concept-seed --scope direction --mode operate) assigned position 4, Flight Strip Board; the user chose this
direction, shown as Impeccable's pick, over the assigned one.

SIGNATURE: Opportunity Board re-rank. Moving a weight re-flows the ranked rows with FLIP motion while stacked
contribution bars morph; charts draw in once from their baseline. Routine transitions 150-250ms.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
